# TBO Theme

Team Back Office colours for TBO Copilot.

| Theme | Accent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **TBO Dark** | `#FB991C`, the brand orange | Dark text on orange and orange text on the dark background are both 8.2:1 |
| **TBO Light** | `#A85A00`, a darker shade of the brand orange | The brand orange on white is only 2.2:1; `#A85A00` is 5.1:1 with white text and 4.8:1 on the light surfaces |

Only the accent tokens (`--ds-accent`, `--ds-accent-hover`, `--ds-accent-soft`) change: buttons, focus rings, selection and highlights turn orange. Backgrounds and text stay PI-Desktop's neutral palette.

**Choosing it:** Settings → General → Theme → **TBO Light** or **TBO Dark**.
