# Report a problem with TBO Copilot

When TBO Copilot gets something wrong, report it. Each report becomes a task for the TBO Copilot team. This matters most during the pilot.

## 1. How

| Where | What to do |
|---|---|
| Teams | Write `report a problem:` and then what happened, in your chat with TBO Copilot. `feedback:` works too |
| The TBO Copilot panel in TBO Support | The same: `report a problem: …` |
| The desktop app | Command menu → **TBO Support: Report a problem**, then write what happened |

**A good report says:**
- what you asked or pressed;
- what you expected;
- what happened instead;
- the ticket or task number, if there was one.

*Example:* `report a problem: I asked for the status of acme.tbo365.cloud and it showed demo.tbo365.cloud's backup instead.`

**If you write only "report a problem"** (or complain in other words), Copilot does not file anything. It shows you how to report, so nothing is filed you did not ask for.

## 2. What happens

1. Copilot creates a task straight away:
   - **subject:** "Copilot feedback: " and your first line;
   - **category:** Development, priority Medium;
   - **project:** the one set in HDS Copilot Settings → **Feedback tasks go to** (else the hub's internal project).
2. It answers with the task's number and a link to it in My Work.
3. The TBO Copilot team reads every report during the pilot. They either fix it, or say why not on the task.

## 3. What the task holds

**It holds:**
- your words;
- who you are, and where you wrote it (Teams, the panel, the desktop app);
- your last 5 Copilot actions, by time, channel, tool, status and record number only.

**It never holds:**
- the content of those actions;
- what a ticket says;
- any customer's data.

**So:**
- Do not paste a customer's personal data, passwords or keys into a report.
- Give the ticket or task number instead. The team can open it with their own permissions.

## 4. Limits

**10 reports an hour per person.** If you hit the limit, put the rest in one report.

## 5. For the TBO Copilot team

- **Where:** reports are ordinary tasks in the feedback project. Triage them on the task board.
- **Numbers:** the Monday pilot email counts them per person in its **Copilot use** table.
- **Hub details:** the hub side is in helpdesk's `docs/tbo-copilot.md`, section **Pilot readiness**.
